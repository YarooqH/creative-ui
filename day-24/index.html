Paper sampler

Six sheets, folded in three. Scroll to open them.

No. 01

Kami

70 gsm

Kami

Thin, crisp and coloured on one side. The paper most cranes are folded from: it holds a sharp crease and forgives very little.

No. 02

Kraft

120 gsm

Kraft

Unbleached wood pulp, strong enough for parcels. The long fibres make it stubborn to fold, and the creases stay soft and round.

No. 03

Washi

40 gsm

Washi

Made from kōzo bark, with fibres you can see when you hold it to the light. It tears instead of cutting, and folds like cloth.

No. 04

Tant

100 gsm

Tant

Lightly embossed, so it grips under the thumb. The colour runs right through, which means the edges of a fold never show white.

No. 05

Glassine

40 gsm

Glassine

Pressed until it is smooth, shiny and almost see-through. It keeps grease and damp out, and every fold leaves a white scar.

No. 06

Cotton rag

300 gsm

Cotton rag

Thick, soft and heavy, made from cotton linters. Too heavy to fold small, so it is scored first and bent just once.
